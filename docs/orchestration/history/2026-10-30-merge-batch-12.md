## What changed and why

**M11's pack half exists.** Before this landing the protocol carried no
pack frame on either enum and `docs/status.md` said so; `main` now has
the frames, the hub's registry, the runner's content-addressed store and
the peer leg, so **E-12 and E-17 have a product behind them** and M11 is
no longer half a milestone. It is the batch D199 reserved all three
public version numbers for, and it spends all three in one landing:
`PROTOCOL_VERSION` 2 → **3**, `RECORD_VERSION` 1 → **2**,
`LAYOUT_VERSION` 1 → **2**. D199's ordering constraint is thereby spent,
and its clause about *"the ordinary release before v1.0.0"* is recorded
**moot** rather than conflicting (D243).

What `main` can do that it could not: accept a pack registration keyed on
`(commit, key)` and refuse two ids for one key **by name** rather than
picking one; lay a pack into a job as hard links so a 400 MiB pack costs
directory entries and not bytes; serve and fetch a pack's objects between
peers on the one socket invariant 3 permits, taking D10's paths in D10's
order and **reporting which one it took**; and refuse, at the hub's
store, both an escaping manifest path where a collection is judged whole
and a chunk whose record names no digest. It also now **sees a
compile-time skip**: 38 `#[ignore]` attributes that were a silent
exemption from D27 are a named list the gate compares.
