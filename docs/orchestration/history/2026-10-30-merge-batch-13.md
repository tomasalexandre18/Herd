## What changed and why

This batch moves no milestone. It moves the **evidence** behind two of
them, and it corrects two sentences `main` was carrying.

What `main` can do now that it could not before:

- **It fails if a figure in a shipped `--help` disagrees with the
  constant that decides it.** All three binaries gained
  `the_long_help_states_every_figure_its_constants_decide`, 21 pairings,
  each figure read back through its own formatter and never written out a
  second time (**D245**). `--help` was the first operator surface anybody
  reads and the only one in the repository with no gate of any kind:
  `herd-runner --help` had described `--job-output-cap` as *"32 MiB by
  default, which is also the most this can be"* against a constant of
  `1 << 30`, and that sentence shipped in v0.3.7, v0.4.0 and v0.4.1
  (ORCH-198).
- **Four of the startup audit's six untested failure modes are tested**,
  through real binaries on both sides where the fixture allowed it: the
  real runner's barrier against a hub whose `readdir` fails (F1,
  ORCH-197); the hub's startup sweep asserted at its own entry point
  rather than on an already-open store (F2); a swept collection on a
  **recreated state root** refused, which is the only wrong-bytes failure
  mode on the audit's whole list (F3); and the hub displacing a runner
  that has gone quiet on an **open** link, which had no test of any kind
  (F4). F5, F6 and F7 are owed and ranked (ORCH-228).
- **One digest rule has one wording, in three readers rather than two.**
  `herd-runner`'s `packstore::object_name` now delegates to
  `herd_proto::pack::check_object_digest`, and a **third** hand-rolled
  copy nobody had counted was found and folded: `herd-hub`'s
  `registry.rs` `looks_like_an_id`, the `packs/manifests/` name filter,
  which had no test and now has one proven red (ORCH-218, invariant 4).
- **`registry.rs` says what is true about its holdings**, and **D246**
  says it in the record: D231 decision 5 is a **design**, not this
  build's behaviour.
- **The `;`-versus-`&&` rule is written down**
  (`gotchas/a-gate-joined-by-a-semicolon-is-advice.md`), with its verify
  step run both ways. It earned its keep hours after being written.
