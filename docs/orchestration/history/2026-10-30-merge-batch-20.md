## What changed and why

**M12 gets its first eviction trigger, its first locality term and its
first pack figure an operator can read — and none of the three removes
a byte or moves a must.** R1 gives the hub a third disk figure,
`--volume-free-floor`, with no default: when it is set the sweeper's
ceiling becomes `min(B, held + free − R)`, saturating, derived from
`statvfs` at every trigger rather than once at startup, and a hub that
cannot measure its volume keeps the behaviour of a hub with no floor and
says so exactly once. L2 adds one term to `matcher::place`, between the
declared `preference` and the name: how many of the packs a job's
`needs` resolve to the machine already holds. P2a fills the health
report's pack triple from one route, so a runner now says what its pack
store holds against the budget it was given — and absence keeps its
meaning per field rather than collapsing into a zero.

What `main` can do now that it could not before: start a hub that gives
its own bytes back when the volume it shares runs low, instead of
sitting inside a budget while the filesystem fills (`D254`); place a job
on a runner that already holds what it needs, without a requester ever
naming a machine (`D255`); and read `pack_store_bytes`,
`pack_store_budget_bytes` and `packs_held` off the real wire. The prose
half files M12's missing middle on the board and repairs a gotcha sheet
six documents cite by path.

**No expectation is promoted by this landing.** Nothing evicts a pack,
no requester surface can submit `needs` or `leaves`, and the hub still
discards all three pack figures, so the musts tally stays at 42 of 44
(`D248`) and `docs/status.md` is left exactly as it stands — nothing in
it is made false by this batch, which *Left out* measures line by line.
