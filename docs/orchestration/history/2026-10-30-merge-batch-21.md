## What changed and why

**M12's pack half gains its eviction mechanism and its first figures an
operator can read, and the two meet in the middle: the gc exists and
nothing calls it, while the hub can now say what every runner holds.**
This moves M12 (track P) and touches **E-16** — a runner's declared
capacity read back honestly — and nothing else of the coverage table.

`main` can now do two things it could not: a runner's `PackStore` can be
brought back under a byte budget by taking **whole packs**, in an order
that never touches what a queued or running job references and never
touches what the keeper pins, with a manifest and its objects leaving
together or not at all; and `herd-hub status`, `status.json` and
`herd-hub report` all say what a runner's pack store holds against its
budget, with *the runner did not say* a distinct state from zero and
from *no budget*.

What it still cannot do: **spend a budget**. `evict_to_budget` has no
caller in the crate, deliberately, so a runner is bounded only by its
disk exactly as it was before this batch. That is ORCH-264, filed here.
