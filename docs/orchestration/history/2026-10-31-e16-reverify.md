### Hub's disk versus a runner's data root

**The distinction the row's note did not make, and the one that matters
to the consumer.** Clauses 1 to 8 above are **all** about a runner's
data root. The hub's side of E-16's note is a different disk and a
different binary:

- `--output-budget` (200 GB default) and `--output-floor` (seven days)
  bound the **collected output files in the hub's own state root**, and
  the flag's own doc says *"the output files only, not the state root"*
  (`herd-hub/src/main.rs:180-199`). M11, **D152**.
- `--volume-free-floor` (**no default**, `herd-hub/src/main.rs:241`)
  measures free space **on the filesystem the hub's state root sits on**
  at every trigger, and removes a job's output files although the budget
  still has room (**D213**, ORCH-095, the R1 work).
- `herd-hub report` section 5 weighs the hub's records, event files and
  logs (**D271**); section 4 weighs the hub's own `packs/`.

**None of the three reads or bounds a runner's disk.** Measured, with
the detector proven on a known-positive first: `statvfs` matches **10**
times under `herd-hub/src/` and **0** times anywhere under
`herd-runner/src/` — there is no free-space guard on a runner at all.
Likewise `spool` and `outputs/` match **0** times in
`herd-hub/src/status.rs` and `herd-hub/src/report.rs`, where `pack`
matches 130 and 115 — so a runner's output spools appear in no document
and no report the hub prints.
