## What changed and why

**No milestone moves and no `E-nn` State cell changes.** `docs/coverage.md`
is untouched by this batch, so nothing here moves delivery or scope, and
**this record writes no tally** for that reason. What moves is the
*memory arithmetic a hub and a runner share*, one test's premise, and the
truthfulness of one landed record.

`main` can now say three things it could not. A **runner** admits while
`Σ granted ≤ lent − floor` rather than `seats × --job-memory-max ≤ lent −
floor` — the same bound at finer granularity, so invariant 2's exposure
does not move — and keeps the count as the bound a host that will not say
its size has. A **hub** charges `herd_proto::charged_ceiling_mib`, the
ceiling the runner will actually grant, bounded by `C_hub := (lent −
floor) / jobs_at_once`, so hub and runner stop counting differently
(**D159**, **D169**) and no attempt is spent on a refusal. And
`a_link_cut_while_the_runner_is_full_loses_no_job` no longer bets on how
fast this host is: its premise is **built** rather than timed, the outage
ending when the hub says it has written every attempt off.

**What this landing does not deliver is the packing gain**, and that is
stated rather than implied: no runner holds more jobs after it than
before. ORCH-323 carries the one thing that would, and the orchestrator
ruled it at this landing — see *Decisions allocated*.
