## What changed and why

**No milestone moves and no `E-nn` State cell changes.** `docs/coverage.md`
is untouched by this batch, so nothing here moves delivery or scope, and
**this record writes no tally** for that reason (**D286**'s rule: a
tally is derived at the tip that carries it, and this tip carries none).

`main` can now say two things it could not, one per branch, and they are
unrelated to each other.

**The hub charges a requirement reading, and the announced count stops
being the bound** (`orch-323-charge-a-reading`, ORCH-323, **D287**).
`herd_hub::matcher::charged_against_the_room` lost its `a_reading`
argument and its `None if a_reading => 0` arm: a job arriving with no
`JobNeed` is charged `C_hub = (lent − floor) ÷ jobs_at_once`, floored at
`MINIMUM_CEILING_MIB`, whether the hub minted it as a reading or a
requester submitted one. The reason is that the **runner cannot tell
them apart** — `job_ceiling` grants every figureless job the whole of
`--job-memory-max` and nothing on the frame says which jobs are
readings — so a hub charging one of the two less was charging a figure
its runner would not honour. With one charge over one set on both sides,
`admission` no longer holds `jobs_at_once` against `seat.reserved.jobs`
in the general case, and that is the packing D285 said the previous
landing had not delivered: **22 jobs declaring 1 GiB now fit on a seat
admitting 25 GiB that announced two places**, where two fit before and
23 GiB of what it lends went unused. **`PROTOCOL_VERSION` stays 3** — no
frame, no field, no changed field meaning — verified at the integration
tip and not taken on trust.

**A skip total stops being a figure anybody maintains**
(`orch-313-skip-count-sweep`, ORCH-313). Eighteen sentences across seven
files named a skip **count**; they now name the list in
`scripts/expected-skips/` or derive the figure from it. This is D187 and
D27 applied to the prose that describes them: a count is not a signal a
reader can reason about and a list is, and the sweep exists because
batch 31 falsified two counted sentences by adding one name. Both script
edits are **line-count-neutral on purpose** — `scripts/gate.sh` is 733
lines and `scripts/test.sh` 302 on both sides — because `board.md` and
the gotchas point at `gate.sh:150`-`:152`, `:160`, `:240`, `:244`,
`:278` and `test.sh:90`, `:149`, `:160`, and a resolution that moved
either count would have silently falsified up to eight `file:line`
pointers. **Both counts were re-measured at the integration tip**, not
assumed from the brief.
