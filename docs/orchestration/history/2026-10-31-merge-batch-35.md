## What changed and why

**A live data-loss defect on v1.0.0, reported by the first consumer on
2026-10-31 and fixed here.** Their runner's link dropped about 71 s at
host load 172. A job finished inside that window, the runner held its
result, the verdict arrived on the next link — and **17 output files,
42 MiB, never came home**, while the hub's record read `finished,
exited 0` and `herdctl` exited 2 with 17 "did NOT get" lines.

The hub asked for owed collections once per connection, at join, from
`ask_for_the_files_still_owed`, and its own comment says that frame
goes out *before the hub reads a frame*. At that instant the record
carried no manifest, so `collection_owed` answered `None` and the hub
named nothing; the manifest was written milliseconds later by the
re-offered verdict. `Session::attach` deliberately does not restart the
runner's pump and the held-verdict loop only ever gives up and
reports, so **nothing asked again**.

**What `main` can do now that it could not before.** The hub asks the
moment it learns there is a collection: in the `ToHub::JobFinished`
arm, after `record_the_end` has written the manifest, it asks
`JobStore::collection_owed` for that one job and sends one
`FromHub::JobFilesHeld` when the collection is owed by the runner that
spoke, guarded on the frame (`outputs.streamed` and a non-empty
`outputs.files`) so an ordinary verdict reads no record. It is the join
scan's own derivation asked for one job — D79's one definition asked a
third way — so there is no second rule to keep in agreement with the
scan, and the scan's ordering becomes **structurally irrelevant**
rather than merely tightened. Hub only: no frame changed,
`PROTOCOL_VERSION` unmoved, and `herd-proto`, `herd-runner` and
`herdctl` untouched.

No milestone row moves and no `E-nn` expectation is newly met: this is
a correctness fix inside M11's collection path, not new scope.
