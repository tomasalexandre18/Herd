## What changed and why

**No milestone moves and no `E-nn` is delivered**: this batch buys two
detectors and no product behaviour. `main` can do two things it could
not before, and each is its own branch's.

**One — a published record format is held by the compiler.**
`events.jsonl` has been a published compatibility surface since D8, and
until this batch nothing in the tree could fail when its bytes changed:
the whitelist at `herd-hub/tests/announced_job_ceiling.rs:396`-`:417`
enumerated **eight** event kinds and was green on 2026-10-31 with
**nine** in the tree, because it only ever saw the lines one scenario
happened to produce. `herd-hub/src/store/frozen_events.rs` replaces it
with fourteen byte-frozen lines written by the hub's own writer, a decode
leg that reads the same text back through `job_events`, and **exhaustive
matches** over `JobEventKind` and `JobState` naming every field of every
variant with no `..` and no `_` arm. A tenth kind, a field added to a
variant and a field taken out of one are each a **compile error**.
D292's extension of `RECORD_VERSION`'s additive rule from fields to
variants is now written in `RECORD_VERSION`'s doc, in `JobEventKind`'s
doc under *Adding a kind here*, in `docs/architecture.md` and in
`docs/coverage.md`'s E-53 cell. No byte of any record changed and no
version number moved.

**Two — a board row has one shape and the gate requires it.** All three
row-level checks in `check-docs.sh` anchored on the literal `^| ORCH-`,
so their blind set was every line that renders as a row of that table
and does not begin with exactly those characters. `check-docs.sh` now
**requires** the canonical `| ORCH-<n> | ` rather than forbidding one
bad spelling, with ten cases in `check-orchestration.sh` proven live by
mutation.
