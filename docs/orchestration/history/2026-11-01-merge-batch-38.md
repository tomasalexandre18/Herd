## What changed and why

M11, and the second half of a **live v1.0.0 defect** the first consumer
measured on 2026-10-31. `main` can now tell two refusals apart that
`herd-proto` said in writing were the same fact: a runner at the ceiling
it announced, and a runner refusing a **second attempt of a job it is
already running**. `ToHub::AttemptAbandoned` gains a second
`#[serde(default)]` field, `still_running_attempt`; the hub excludes that
one `(job, runner)` pair until the machine's next health report stops
naming the job, and the matcher answers `ShortNow`, so the job **waits
for the place it is already in** instead of dying. No
`PROTOCOL_VERSION` move, no `RECORD_VERSION` move, no new frame, no
clock.

**What the release may promise, and it is less than the mechanism
suggests. The defect is bounded, not removed.** The exclusion lives on
the **connection**: `peers.runners.insert` hands every redial a fresh
empty set, and `dispatch_queued_jobs` runs **before**
`relay_from_runner`, so the hub offers on the hello. That is **one
self-collision refusal per connection, re-armed by a redial** — so
**eight redials during one long attempt still end the job
`refused_as_full`, exit 125, with no verdict**. What is closed is the
0.51 s of them that killed `j0000000655`. `herd-reviewer` found this;
three artefacts had overstated it as *"the storm is closed"* and all
three are corrected.

**The upgrade order is runner first, and a hub deployed alone fixes
nothing** — the reverse of D181. The new field's absence at an
un-upgraded v1.0.0 runner is an **absence of testimony**, not a denial:
that runner refuses in exactly this way and sends no key. The decision
stands — a misread degrades to today's behaviour, and there is no
compatibility to preserve — but the ground is that, not the version
doc's usual one, and the cost is carried named rather than softened, on
D292's precedent.
