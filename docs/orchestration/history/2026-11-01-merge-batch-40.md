## What changed and why

**A method change, not a milestone.** `main` gains no capability: this batch
contains no Rust, no `Cargo.toml` byte, no script and no workflow. What it
gains is one corrected sentence of the contract.

`CLAUDE.md`'s *Commands* paragraph exempted the local Rust gates **by
directory** — `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `docs/`, `.claude/`, `.github/*.md` —
and `deploy/` was not on the list. By its letter a markdown-only change under
`deploy/` owed all four Rust gates locally although **no Rust gate reads any
file under `deploy/`**. v1.1.0's cut hit the case exactly: it ran
`check-docs.sh` alone and reported the four as not run, which was right under
the letter as it stood. The rule now exempts **only the markdown under
`deploy/` and nothing else there** (Alexa, 2026-11-01, ORCH-357).

**The boundary is the decision, not the exemption.** The nine non-markdown
files under `deploy/` keep the obligation the previous letter gave them, and
the clause makes no statement about any other extension. A wholesale
`deploy/` exemption was not authorised and is not in this landing.
