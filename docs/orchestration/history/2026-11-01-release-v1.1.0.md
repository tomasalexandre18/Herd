## Risks and findings

### 1. The warrant for leaving `PROTOCOL_VERSION` at 3 is refuted

The brief states that `PROTOCOL_VERSION` *"has not moved since the
workspace's first commit — I checked its whole history with `git log
-S`"*. **It has moved, twice, and the measurement cannot see it.**

`git log -S'pub const PROTOCOL_VERSION: u32'` returns exactly one
commit, `12cca8e add the four-crate workspace and herd-proto v1`. That
is not evidence of a constant that never moved: `-S` reports commits
where the **count of occurrences** of the string changed, and a string
that stops before the `= 3` has the same count on both sides of a value
change. The detector is blind to the only event it was pointed at.

**Proven, on the values:** `git show v0.4.1:herd-proto/src/lib.rs`
through `release.yml`'s own `sed` gives **2**; at `v1.0.0` and at
`origin/main` it gives **3**. `d114f03 move the protocol to 2` and
`a99dfa9 Move PROTOCOL_VERSION to 3 and number the pack types` are both
ancestors of `main` and both say so in their subjects. The constant's own
doc comment at `:135` opens *"What is NOT foreclosed … D204's stability
promise constrains the feature surface, not this constant"* — which is a
sentence written **about** a bump that had just happened.

**The conclusion is unaffected and I did not bump it.** The question is
not whether the number ever moved; it is whether anything in these 101
commits requires it to move, and the answer is no: `3` at `v1.0.0` and
`3` at `origin/main`, and both new fields are `#[serde(default)]` on
existing variants, the first of the three shapes the doc comment blesses.
**Invariant 4 is not breached and I am not stopping over it.** I am
reporting it because a true instruction resting on a measurement that
cannot support it will be reused, and the next constant it is pointed at
may be one that did move.

**Drafted as a decision below**, because this is the third time in three
days this project has written down a rule about a detector's warrant
(D286, D288, D293) and this one is cheap to state.

### 2. The figureless-reading charge is D285 **and D287**, not D285/D286.

The brief cites item 3 as *"ORCH-323, my ruling, D285/D286"*. **D286 is
*"D280's closing tally was stale on arrival"*** — a correction to a
coverage tally, with nothing to do with charging. The ruling is in
**D285** (*"option (b) — a figureless requirement reading is charged
`C_hub`"*) and its implementation is **D287** (*"The hub charges a
reading, and the count stops being the bound"*, which opens *"ORCH-323,
and the second half of D285"*). The body cites D285 and D287. Nothing
else in the brief's list mis-cites.

### 3. The next action the brief proposed for the hazard cannot be performed.

The brief asks that the mixed-fleet hazard be measured *"against a 0.4.1
runner"*. **A v0.4.1 runner cannot reach admission on a v1.1.0 hub at
all:** it announces `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 2 against this hub's 3, and the
check is **equality, not a floor** —
`herd_proto::version_mismatch_reason` says so in the sentence it hands
the operator, *"a peer and a hub on two different numbers never speak"*.
It is refused at the handshake and never gets as far as a refusal storm.

## 2026-11-01 — D-NEXT A pickaxe over a constant's name cannot see

**Context.** The brief for `cut-v1.1.0` instructed that
`PROTOCOL_VERSION` not be bumped and gave as its warrant that the

warrant was false, and the two are independent.

**The measurement, and why it reads as a zero.**
`git log -S'pub const PROTOCOL_VERSION: u32'` returns exactly one
commit, `12cca8e`, the one that introduced the line. `-S` reports a
commit when the **count of occurrences** of the string changes. A
pattern that stops before the value has the same count on both sides of
`= 2` → `= 3`, so the pickaxe is silent across precisely the event it
was aimed at. It answers *"was this declaration added or removed"* and
was read as answering *"did this number ever change"*.

**What is true.** Through `release.yml`'s own `sed`,
`herd-proto/src/lib.rs` gives **2** at `v0.4.1` and **3** at `v1.0.0`
and at `main`. Both moves name themselves in a subject line —
`d114f03 move the protocol to 2`, `a99dfa9 Move PROTOCOL_VERSION to 3
and number the pack types` — and the constant's own doc comment at
`:135` is written *about* the second one.

**The rule.** A claim that a constant has not moved is made by **reading
its value at the two ends** — `git show <ref>:<file>` through the same
expression the consumer of the number uses — and never by a pickaxe over
its name. Where a pickaxe is the right instrument, the pattern carries
the value (`-S'… = 3;'`), and the result is read as a change in a count
of occurrences rather than as a history of the number. This is the same
shape as **D293**: a detector is trusted only once it has been shown to
fire on the case it is meant to catch, and an unproven detector's
silence is not a measurement.

### 1. Is this 1.1.0 or 2.0.0? The question is about a promise

**Question.** `herd_proto::version_gap` refuses a connection whose peer
is a **major** version away and **serves a minor one with a note**. Its

Herd's own behaviour is allowed to change under an unchanged protocol —

runner bounds itself by changes, and what a refusal means changes — all

today's evidence. And the minor is where the brief's own reasoning puts
