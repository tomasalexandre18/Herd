## What changed and why

**No milestone moves and no capability is added.** This batch is two
corrections to things Herd *says*, one in prose and one in a program, both
out of one operator confusion on the first consumer's machines, and the board
rows that keep the class visible.

What `main` can do now that it could not before: **tell an operator the truth
about a runner that stopped.** `herdctl` previously printed
`; it is also stopping (SIGTERM)` for any runner the hub reported as
stopping, although `RunnerSwitch.stopping` is a `bool` that **every** stop
path sets — the signal and the `drain` position that has emptied and leaves by
itself alike. The parenthetical was a literal. The line now says
`; it is also stopping` and names no cause (**D297**); the cause is
deliberately **not** inferred from the `switch` beside it, because a runner
that is draining *and* was signalled is one machine with two causes.

And `docs/joining.md` no longer contradicts itself on one screen: `:740` read
*"Neither position stops the runner"* eleven lines under the table row saying
a `drain` *"leaves once it holds nothing"*, and the half a reader reaches
second was the false one. An operator chose `drain` on that sentence.

**Measured end to end, not inferred.** At 01:11:04 CET on 2026-11-02 the
consumer's server runner journalled `draining (the owner's switch is draining
(hub upgrade to 1.1.0 …) and this runner holds nothing, so it leaves); 0
job(s) to end` — their own excerpt of `herd-runner/src/lib.rs:4305` — their
unit's `Restart` started it again, `at_startup` read the persisted `drain` as
`off` (**D164**), and they came to us asking what had restarted their runner.
`0 job(s) to end`: **nothing was in flight and no work was lost.** The defect
is that our document let them pick the position that exits and our program
then blamed a signal nobody had sent.

**No wire change and no version move.** `herd-proto` is touched by doc
comment only, `PROTOCOL_VERSION` stays **3**, and `RECORD_VERSION` and
`LAYOUT_VERSION` are untouched. Whether the stop's *reason* should travel to
the operator is priced across three hops and **not decided here** (ORCH-363).
